FAQ

Clear answers before you start.

Do you sell reviews?

No. This site is intentionally built as a compliant alternative. It helps businesses ask real customers for honest reviews and improve service recovery.

Can we ask only happy customers?

No. The safest workflow is to request honest feedback from real customers without conditioning the request on a positive rating.

Can this remove bad reviews?

No platform removal is guaranteed. The platform helps you respond, document issues, and recover customer relationships where possible.

Is this useful for agencies?

Yes. Agencies can manage review requests, reporting, and response workflows for multiple client locations.
