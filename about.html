Agency Story

PRaakar grows from a communication legacy that began with KS Public Relations and expanded into a broader integrated agency model.

From KSPR to PRaakar Communications LLP

With more than 14 years of communication experience behind it, PRaakar was shaped to meet a wider set of business needs than traditional PR alone. The agency combines press visibility, advertising support, digital reach, messaging, and regional campaign execution.

That shift matters because most businesses do not need isolated communication activity. They need one partner who can understand the brief, align channels, and help the brand show up consistently in the right markets.

What defines the agency

Integrated PR, advertising, digital, and content support

Stronger regional execution across Madhya Pradesh and beyond

A practical, business-first approach to visibility

Campaign planning built for launches, reach, and recall
